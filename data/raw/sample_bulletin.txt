PAGASA SEVERE WEATHER BULLETIN #15
Typhoon "SAMPLE" (SAMPLE)
Issued at 5:00 AM, 17 November 2024

"SAMPLE" intensified into a Super Typhoon while moving west-northwestward
over the Philippine Sea east of Northern Luzon.

HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND AREAS
Super Typhoon SAMPLE will bring heavy rainfall and destructive winds to
Northern Luzon in the next 72 hours. Tropical cyclone wind signal (TCWS)
#4 is hoisted over Batanes, Cagayan, and Isabela. TCWS #3 over Apayao,
Ilocos Norte, and Abra.

TRACK AND INTENSITY OUTLOOK
SAMPLE is forecast to move west-northwestward and exit the Philippine
Area of Responsibility by Thursday evening.

CURRENT WEATHER SYSTEMS
The southwest monsoon enhanced by SAMPLE will bring rains over the western
sections of Central and Southern Luzon.

LOCATION OF CENTER (as of 5:00 AM)
15.2N, 124.8E
Moving: West-northwestward at 20 km/h
Central pressure: 915 hPa
Maximum sustained winds: 195 km/h near the center, with gustiness of up
to 240 km/h
